## Cyber Talk - Digital Forensics Workshop

Date: 10-5-2026
Venue: ICT TL-E5-01, KICT

This workshop is designed for beginners who want to get started with digital forensics, cybersecurity, and Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions. No previous CTF experience is required.

Digital forensics is the art of recovering and analyzing evidence from digital sources. In CTF competitions, forensics challenges train participants to extract hidden information from various sources like image metadata, network packet captures, steganography, and disk images.
